Why 20 minutes

Why 20 minutes?

Fall into deep sleep, and you wake heavy. Twenty minutes is the optimal rhythm to rest the brain just before deep sleep sets in, restoring cognitive function. Within that light recovery, thinking is reset — so you can start moving again, clear and refreshed.
