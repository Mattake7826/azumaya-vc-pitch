Problem

Meeting rooms and focus spaces keep multiplying. Yet places designed to step away, even briefly, from outside stimulation and deliberately reset the mind are still rare.

Azumaya Experience

20 minutes away

Azumaya is a personal recovery space where you step away, briefly, from outside noise and sightlines to settle your mind and brain in a space of your own.

The Product

This is Azumaya.

A mesh structure built for lightness creates a personal space that gently envelops you.

Scale prototype (additive manufacturing)

  • A structure built for lightness

    A mesh frame that forms shape from lines rather than panels, cutting weight while keeping sufficient strength.

  • A personal space that envelops you

    The egg-shaped frame gently surrounds the body, creating a space of your own that feels shielded from outside eyes.

  • Designed to step away from the outside world

    The structure softens sound and light, temporarily reducing the flow of information around you.

  • A recovery space built for short use

    Designed not for long stays, but for a short 20-minute reset.
